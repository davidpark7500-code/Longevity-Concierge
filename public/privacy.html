Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Longevity Concierge ("we," "us") is operated by David Park, Raymore, Missouri. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Longevity Concierge web app and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and first name.
  • What you log: habit check-ins, meals, protein and fiber totals, favorites, and any personal targets you enter.
  • Coach: messages you send to the AI habit coach.
  • Community: posts, cheers and challenge check-ins you share.
  • Package details: which package you have or asked about, so your concierge can help you. Orders and payments happen with Amway, not in the app.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your data across your devices, provide coach replies, run the community, help your concierge support your package, send sign-in emails, and keep the service safe. We do not sell your data.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (hosting), Resend (sign-in emails), and Anthropic (AI coach; messages you send to the coach go to Anthropic to generate a reply). Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Health-related information

Food logs and habits can reveal information about your health. We treat them as sensitive, use them only to provide the app to you, and will notify you as required by law, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule, if they are ever accessed without authorization.

Coach safety reviews

To keep the coach within general wellness topics, replies that are blocked by our safety check are saved so our team can review them.

Your choices

You can edit your name in the app, and delete your account and all your data at any time from Account → Delete my account. Questions or requests: hello@longevityconcierge.app.

Children

The app is for adults 18 and older.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for material changes, let you know in the app.
