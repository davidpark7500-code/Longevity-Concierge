Delete your Longevity Concierge account

Longevity Concierge by Holistic Creative Studios LLC

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Longevity Concierge and sign in.
  2. Tap your name at the top right to open Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email hello@longevityconcierge.app from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account, name and email, habit and food logs, coach history, community posts, cheers, challenge check-ins and block list. Copies of posts that others reported may be kept for up to 90 days for safety records, then deleted.

Product orders placed with your concierge are separate from the app and are not affected.
