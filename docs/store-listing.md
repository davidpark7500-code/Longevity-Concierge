```text
Longevity Concierge is a free habit and accountability app for people who want to build steady, everyday wellness habits, and who do better with a little support.

Most of us know what we should be doing. The hard part is doing it again tomorrow. Longevity Concierge keeps it simple: a few habits, one tap each, and people in your corner.

TODAY: FIVE SIMPLE HABITS
- Protein with each meal
- Hydration
- Strength or movement
- Fiber
- Wind down on time
Tap to check each one off, watch your daily ring fill up, and keep your streak going.

FOOD LOG: PROTEIN AND FIBER
Type what you ate and see the protein and fiber add up against your daily targets. Save favorites for quick logging, look back over the week, and use the protein and fiber finder to compare thousands of foods using USDA data.

LEARN

CHECK-IN: COMMUNITY AND CHALLENGES

HABIT COACH

DAILY REMINDER
Turn on a gentle daily reminder to check in on your habits, at the time that works for you.

Your concierge is a real person you can text with questions or when you feel stuck.

NUTRITION PACKAGES AND THE CIRCLE (OPTIONAL)

The app itself is free. There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

YOUR DATA
Sign in with a code sent to your email, with no password to remember. Your habits and food log sync across your devices. We do not sell your data, and you can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

Longevity Concierge is published by Holistic Creative Studios LLC.
```

## App Privacy (Apple) and Data safety (Google)

Based only on `public/privacy.html` and the code (`src/main.js`, `src/cloud.js`, `src/native.js`, `supabase/functions/api`). Service providers: Supabase (database, sign-in, synced app state), Vercel (hosting), Resend (sign-in emails), Anthropic (AI coach replies). No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs were found in the code.

### What is collected

| Data | Apple type | Google type | Linked to user | Tracking | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Contact Info > Email Address | Personal info > Email address | Yes | No | App functionality (sign-in codes, account) | Required |
| First name | Contact Info > Name | Personal info > Name | Yes | No | App functionality (greeting, shown on community posts) | Required at onboarding |
| Account ID (Supabase user ID) | Identifiers > User ID | Personal info > User IDs | Yes | No | App functionality | Required |
| Habit check-ins, food entries, protein and fiber totals, personal targets, favorites | Health & Fitness > Health and Fitness | Health and fitness > Health info, Fitness info | Yes | No | App functionality (sync across devices) | Optional (the user chooses what to log) |
| Community posts, cheers, challenge check-ins | User Content > Other User Content | Messages > Other in-app messages; App activity > Other user-generated content | Yes | No | App functionality (community) | Optional |
| Messages to the AI habit coach | User Content > Other User Content | App activity > Other user-generated content | Yes | No | App functionality (sent to Anthropic to generate a reply; replies blocked by the safety check are stored with the question for review) | Optional |
| Coach usage counts (messages per day) | Usage Data > Product Interaction | App activity > App interactions | Yes | No | App functionality (daily coach limit) | Required if the coach is used |
| Package requested / assigned | Purchases > Purchase History (see flag) | Financial info > Purchase history (see flag) | Yes | No | App functionality (so the concierge can help) | Optional |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos (meal-photo AI is switched off in code: `aiOn = false`), browsing history, device identifiers for ads, payment details, diagnostics.

**Tracking (Apple):** No. Nothing is shared with data brokers or used for ads.

**Google extra answers**
- Data encrypted in transit: Yes (HTTPS to Supabase, Vercel, Resend, Anthropic).
- Users can request deletion: Yes, in the app (Account > Delete my account). Google also requires a **web link** where users can request deletion without the app (see open questions).
- Data shared with third parties: No. Transfers to service providers that process data on our behalf do not count as "sharing" under Google's definition.
- Independent security review: No.

### Flags (uncertain, decide before submitting)

1. **Package details as "Purchase History".** The app stores which package a user asked for or has (`requested_tier`, `tier`), but no purchase happens in the app. It is safer to declare it as Purchase History than to leave it out. Confirm.
2. **Concierge/admin access.** Admins and concierges can see members' first name, email, package and group in the admin screen. That is still "app functionality" and not third-party sharing, but the privacy policy should say concierges on the user's team can see this. The admin API does not read habit or food logs (`user_state`); confirm the database rules keep it that way.
3. **Referral code.** A join-link code (`ref`) is saved at sign-up to assign the user to a concierge. It is not personal data on its own, so it is not declared. Confirm.
4. **Stripe code is still present.** `/api/checkout`, `/api/portal`, a Stripe webhook and a `circle_active` field exist, and the delete-account warning says "any paid membership is cancelled". No button in the app calls checkout today. Make sure the store build cannot reach Stripe checkout at all (Apple would treat a paid Circle subscription as an in-app digital purchase), and reword that warning in the app build.
5. **Privacy policy wording.** It says the app is "operated by David Park, Raymore, Missouri" and describes "the Longevity Concierge web app". Update it to name Holistic Creative Studios LLC and cover the iPhone and Android apps (and mention the optional local reminder notification, which stores no data on our servers) before submitting.

## Open questions

1. **The Circle and Apple 3.1.** The Circle (private group, weekly live calls, members-only challenges) is digital content unlocked by buying a physical package outside the app. Apple may see this as digital access sold outside IAP, and weekly group calls are one-to-many live services, which Apple says must use IAP when sold. Options: describe the Circle as a free benefit included with the physical order (as drafted), or make the Circle available to everyone in the store build. Expect questions from review either way.
2. **Visible disclaimer mentions hidden topics.** The Plan tab disclaimer (`src/main.js` around line 402) says "...any medication, peptide, or dosage" and "Valdura is a separate service with its own terms." It will show in the store build. Consider a store-build version without "peptide" and "Valdura".
3. **Community reporting and blocking** is not in the code yet (only delete-own-post and admin delete). Apple guideline 1.2 needs it built before submission.
4. **Demo account and code** for review: need a reviewer email and a way to get the sign-in code.
5. **Google web deletion link**: Play requires a URL where users can request account deletion outside the app (a section on the privacy page or a simple form is enough).
6. **Naming the product company.** The listing does not name the company that sells the packages. Decide whether the review note should name it.
7. **Age rating 18+**: confirm you want to force 18+ in both stores.
8. **Support URL** points to holisticcreativestudios.com. Make sure that page has a contact method (Apple checks this).
